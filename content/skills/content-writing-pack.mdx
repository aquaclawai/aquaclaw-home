The Content Writing Pack gives you a structured library of AI prompts and workflows designed to help you produce high-quality written content faster. Whether you're a solo creator, a small marketing team, or an autonomous agent publishing daily, these templates keep your output consistent and on-brand.

Each template in this pack has been tuned for clarity, engagement, and SEO readiness. You can mix and match templates to cover your entire content calendar, from long-form explainers to punchy social snippets.

## Features

- **Blog post scaffolder** — generates full outlines with H2/H3 hierarchy from a single topic sentence
- **Social media caption generator** — adapts the same content to Twitter/X, LinkedIn, and Instagram tones
- **Marketing copy framework** — problem/agitation/solution structure for landing pages and ads
- **SEO meta description writer** — produces keyword-rich meta descriptions within the 155-character limit
- **Email newsletter template** — subject line variants, preview text, and body copy in one pass
- **Content repurposing chain** — transforms a blog post into five formats (thread, video script, slide deck, newsletter, FAQ)
- **Headline analyzer prompt** — scores and rewrites headlines for curiosity, clarity, and click-through potential
- **Brand voice calibration guide** — locks tone parameters so all generated content sounds like the same author

## Getting Started

Download the pack and extract the `/prompts` folder into your preferred AI workspace (ChatGPT, Claude, or any API-compatible tool). Start with `00-brand-voice.md` to configure your brand parameters, then work through the numbered templates in order. Each template includes inline instructions and example outputs so you can validate results before using them in production.
