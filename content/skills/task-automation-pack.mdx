The Task Automation Pack gives you a library of prompt templates for identifying, designing, and delegating repetitive work to AI. The templates cover common automation scenarios — inbox management, recurring report generation, meeting prep, and multi-step approval workflows — with instructions for adapting each to your specific tools and team.

This pack focuses on practical, implementable automations rather than theoretical frameworks. Every template has been tested in real operational contexts and produces outputs that can be fed directly into automation platforms like Zapier, Make, or custom API pipelines.

## Features

- **Workflow mapper** — diagrams a manual process as a step-by-step flowchart and identifies automation opportunities
- **Email triage rules generator** — produces label/filter rules and AI-assisted response templates for recurring email types
- **Daily standup summarizer** — aggregates status updates from multiple sources into a structured team briefing
- **Meeting prep assistant** — generates agenda, pre-read summaries, and discussion questions from a meeting title and attendee list
- **Recurring report builder** — creates a repeatable data-to-prose pipeline for weekly and monthly reports
- **Approval workflow designer** — maps decision criteria into a structured routing logic that AI or humans can follow
- **Task prioritization prompt** — applies the Eisenhower matrix (and custom criteria) to a raw task list
- **End-of-day wrap-up generator** — consolidates completed tasks, blockers, and next-day priorities into a shareable summary

## Getting Started

Begin with `workflow-mapper.md` to inventory the manual processes you want to automate. Once you have a candidate workflow, match it to the relevant specialist template (email, reports, scheduling, etc.). Each template includes a "complexity rating" and an estimated time-to-implement so you can prioritize which automations to tackle first. The pack also includes a `tools-compatibility.md` guide mapping each template to popular automation platforms.
